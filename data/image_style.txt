VISUAL STYLE — PREMIUM ISOMETRIC 3D EDITORIAL ILLUSTRATION

Create a refined, premium-quality 3D editorial illustration in the style of a carefully art-directed architectural miniature or product-design diorama.

Rendering and geometry: Use a three-quarter elevated isometric camera angle, precise geometric construction, clean silhouettes, carefully layered forms and convincing physical depth. Combine realistic materials with the clarity and intentional simplification of a high-end miniature model.

Materials: Use tactile, understated materials such as matte ceramic, fine-grain concrete, brushed metal, painted surfaces and subtly textured composites. Maintain realistic material properties, restrained reflections and finely controlled surface detail. Avoid a toy-like plastic appearance.

Lighting: Use soft, diffused studio lighting, gentle directional shadows, realistic ambient occlusion, subtle contact shadows and smooth tonal transitions. Create a quiet, sophisticated atmosphere with excellent separation between foreground objects and background.

Color palette: Use a restrained, low-saturation palette built around warm ivory, greige, pale cool grey, muted stone, deep navy and occasional earthy or desaturated accent colors. Maintain tonal harmony and strong but gentle visual hierarchy.

Composition: Create a balanced, carefully staged composition with one unmistakable focal point. Use a clean background, generous negative space, deliberate spacing and clear separation of visual elements. Keep the primary subject readable at thumbnail size while retaining subtle detail on closer inspection.

Graphic design: Apply premium editorial art direction, precise alignment, minimal graphic overlays, elegant geometric sans-serif typography where required, and thin, understated annotation lines. Keep graphic elements subordinate to the illustration.

Image quality: High-end CGI, physically based rendering, refined global illumination, realistic scale and materials, crisp geometry, subtle micro-texture, soft shadows, controlled highlights, polished commercial-art direction.

Style consistency: Sophisticated, calm, precise, contemporary, minimalist and architectural. The result should resemble a premium design-agency editorial illustration or a professionally photographed miniature installation.

Avoid: Flat cartoon graphics, childish toy aesthetics, exaggerated proportions, excessive saturation, harsh shadows, dramatic cinematic lighting, glossy plastic, cluttered composition, decorative excess, noisy textures, unnecessary visual effects and inconsistent perspective.
